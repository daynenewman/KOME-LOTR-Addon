A. ACTUAL PLAYER JOURNEY
======================================================================

A1. New-world character creation
Action and authority: the server derives race -> gender where selectable ->
starting faction -> appearance -> confirmation from persisted choices.
Orcs/Uruk-hai receive inherent sex and skip the selectable gender step. A
starting allegiance can be applied at finalization when configuration enables
it; selecting a starting faction is not itself a canonical KOME promotion.
Discovery/HUD/book: mandatory native-style creation screens explain this
stage. The later progression HUD/book belongs to the separate KOME progression
record. ESC/pause and reconnect resume the derived mandatory stage.
Indicators/world: race traits, appearance, starting alignment/pledge and the
configured starting waypoint are the material effects; no Master/Liege is
invented by character creation.
Blockers/recovery: invalid race/faction/preset combinations and unfinished
choices prevent finalization; existing choices permit resumption.
Continuity: technically sound, but the handoff from creation to personal
service needs a live first-session test. The two modules have distinct state
authorities, which is appropriate if the player is clearly handed onward.

A4. Serf duties
Action and authority: the current Master issues Provision, Profession and
Courier duties, once each toward this path. All three must be completed.
Ordinary issuance is limited to one task per configured server calendar day.
Provisions request 3-4 distinct goods including native water; foods come from
local/common pools. Profession requests 3-4 nonfood goods from the classified
occupation profile and conservative acquisition allow-list. Small pools may
produce fewer legitimate goods instead of duplicates or invented items.
Discovery/HUD/book: speech announces work, the HUD shows an outstanding goods
line with the native requested item/count, and the book lists the saved
requirements/deposits. Courier instead shows concise delivery/report text
without numeric progress; replacement details remain in the book.
Indicators/world: saved Master/correspondent/objective markers; physical
dispatches and native coin compensation. Incremental goods delivery consumes
real inventory and saves cumulative deposits. A successful direct handover
precedes ordinary NPC GUI opening and uses the native pouch sound.
Blockers/recovery: daily cadence, missing goods, unmatched metadata/vessel,
invalid relationship or missing correspondent. Courier has limited copies
and recovery after confirmed recipient death. Obsolete goods can be replaced
on an explicit Master visit; they are not rerolled on login.
Continuity: this is recognizably household/occupational service. Quantities
and compensation can still make the same duty much more burdensome for one
profession than another. Occupation affects the pool, but not a complete
NPC-specific interpretation of every trade and piece of equipment.

B. DOES THIS FEEL LIKE ONE JOURNEY?
======================================================================

VERIFIED IMPLEMENTATION: the main personal-service spine now has a consistent
logic: household service earns trust; a Lord tests the prospective servant;
that Lord confers Knight standing; commissions demonstrate service; temporary
command tests responsibility for others; the Liege appoints a Lord. Canonical
rank, saved relationships and faction provenance rather than names/checkboxes
authorize these core transitions. Former-Master gifts are independent.

DESIGN CONCERN: three additional systems remain visible around that spine:
legacy advancement permissions, native alignment/miniquests, and strategic
politics/conquest. They can coexist, but the book does not consistently explain
which is mandatory for rank, which unlocks tools, and which is optional flavor.
For example, Knight standing does not automatically unlock faction weapons:
knight.craftsman still unlocks baseline.faction_gear. This is a meaningful
second gate, not merely an old label (AutoCompleter.java:138).

The authority hierarchy is clearer than the content hierarchy. NPC Lord ->
Prince -> King is exact policy; a new player Lord can find a Prince, but there
is no implemented Prince-owned Lord service sequence to justify the search.
The Lord -> Prince marker is still a legacy completion route. The four-soldier
Lordship trial promises leadership; subsequent generic checklist behavior
does not fulfill that promise.

Names now communicate standing publicly. Culture-specific names can differ
from canonical keys: Gondor's canonical Lord is displayed as Captain and
canonical Prince as Lord. This is intentional existing title data, not a
corrupt rank. The UI still needs to make that distinction understandable.
The player formatter avoids "Rider of Rohan of Rohan"; political King office
does not replace the canonical rank in its new suffix.

KNOWN LIMITATION: the full journey is not universal. Fangorn has rank titles
and native pledge support but no native combat-unit-hiring Liege host in this
architecture. A warning is honest; it does not complete that route.

C. PLAYER EXPERIENCE AND FRICTION
======================================================================

Improvements supported by code/tests: text-only courier guidance; actual saved
recovery icons; provision food icons; direct physical handovers; no required
gift collection; current-service filtering; maximum zoom on supported map
targets; once-only promotion transitions; failed-Liege release and explicit
cooldown context; occasional traveler warnings and gratitude.

Remaining friction with grounded causes:
  - Three ordinary Serf duties require at least three configured real calendar
    days. Sleeping through Minecraft nights does not advance that cadence.
    Rejection/generation failure can therefore waste a substantial session.
  - The ordinary duty-complete HUD expires after about five seconds. A player
    returning later may need to reopen the book to learn why no task is visible.
  - Provision/Profession still request 32-64 units for stackable items,
    regardless of economic rarity. Distinct types improve variety, not balance.
  - Quest availability is eligible plus passive-offer-ready on the client,
    whereas some explanatory results describe eligibility alone. An occupied
    native offer or failed offer preparation can leave a disabled button with
    no explanatory string. Existing native quests are correctly preserved.
  - Show Me excludes relationship markers. A return-to-Liege/Master objective
    can therefore lose navigation even though the saved NPC location exists.
  - Native escort pathfinding is still physical. Fences, rivers, unloaded
    routes and fleeing AI are genuine challenges; unreliable navigation is
    accidental frustration, not a test of noble leadership.

LIVE-TESTING REQUIRED: measure the click sequence and dialogue timing with real
native screens, including full inventory, simultaneous client/server actions,
walking NPCs, low FPS and latency. Reading a canceled Forge event in a fixture
does not establish that the player heard and understood the acknowledgment.

D. WORLD BELIEVABILITY AND LOTR AUTHENTICITY
======================================================================

VERIFIED IMPLEMENTATION: native factions, enemy relationships, invasion data,
NPC classes, common crops/materials, native item stacks, speech, hiring AI,
map geometry and coin denominations are reused. Profession classification
recognizes smith/bartender/farmhand interfaces, concrete occupations and native
trade pools before falling back to general labor. Military faction equipment
does not automatically become a Serf supply request.

New structure-dependent objectives require physical roof/support/arrival
evidence and retain it in saved assignments. "The shelter marked on your map"
and "the ground marked on your map" are more defensible than fictitious town
names. No automatic refuge construction was added. Native waypoint or village
prediction data alone is not treated as proof of a standing building.

KNOWN LIMITATION: physical shelter proof is a geometric heuristic. It does
not prove that a structure is native-generated, occupied, culturally matching,
protected by a particular player, or reachable along the intended route. A
roof near a native home witness can be a player construction. These services
do not write blocks, but can still create NPC combat near that witness.

DESIGN CONCERN: most new drinking requests conservatively collapse to water
in ordinary mugs. This is accessible and preferable to unverifiable Miruvor
or specialized brewing, but weakens regional flavor. Native-verified accessible
faction drinks would be a worthwhile later extension.

Curated culture/role templates improve smiths, farmers, travelers and promotion
speech. They do not review every existing sentence into a unique cultural
voice. Human "blessing" gift language remains shared, including nonhuman/evil
cultures. That is a concrete polish gap, not a reason to build a dialogue engine.

No new precise fictional village/person/refuge was found in the new objective
generation. General native-authority guidance names real generator/region
categories, but does not prove a specific structure near the player. Legacy
trial registry prose describing recovery of NPCs/patrols/livestock is stale
relative to the actual bound-item recovery (SerfKnightTrial.java:31).

E. REWARDS, ECONOMY AND INCENTIVES
======================================================================

VERIFIED IMPLEMENTATION: material compensation uses saved per-unit quotes and
actual cumulative delivered counts. Native standard trade entries supply the
cheapest observed per-unit pool price; the reimbursement is one quarter of
that price, rounded down to milli-silver, with at most one silver per unit and
128 silver per duty. Dynamic barrel/enchantment entries are excluded from
ordinary unit valuation. Unknown goods fall back conservatively to 0.01 silver
per unit, or 0.10 for recognized ordinary ingots. No service bonus is added.

Payment is cumulative: floor(total delivered milli-value / 1000), capped at
128, minus already reserved material payment. Fractional value carries across
handovers; replay of the same delivered state has zero new payment. Reservation
precedes physical coin issuance. Active legacy requests acquire quotes without
rerolling their goods/deposit counts; completed legacy rewards receive no new
windfall. Courier/other small secondary rewards retain existing conventions.
Administrative completions carry provenance and do not mint compensation.
Evidence: D/KOMEProgressionGoodsValue.java:17,30,33;
D/KOMEProgressionServiceRewards.java:21;
D/KOMEProgressionGoodsRecovery.java:31.

Concrete calculated examples, using shipped standard trade-bytecode prices:
  Inexpensive builder: 32 cobblestone at 31 milli, 32 oak planks at 62 milli,
  and 32 clay balls at the 10-milli fallback. Total 3,296 milli -> 3 silver.
  Moderate smith: 32 coal at 125 milli, 32 iron ingots at 750 milli, and
  32 copper ingots at 750 milli. Total 52,000 milli -> 52 silver.
  Expensive goldsmith: 64 gold ingots at the 1,000-milli unit cap, 64 gold
  nuggets at 500 milli, 64 copper at 750 milli, and 64 coal at 125 milli.
  Total 152,000 milli -> the 128-silver duty cap.
These are possible request compositions/calculations, not live assignments or
claims about actual player shopping. Native values came from the shipped JAR's
standard entries; runtime matching, discounts and third-party tables need live
confirmation. Diagnostic evidence is under outputs/progression-coherence/.

Exploit assessment: ordinary buy-and-deliver profit is deliberately discouraged
by using a quarter of the lowest standard pool price, even below many native
sale values. Daily restrictions and finite duty/commission credit also remain.
Gathering/farming earns money for genuine supplied resources; that is intended,
not itself money printing. Conversion recipes and third-party discounted/free
trades deserve a targeted market test, particularly ingot/nugget conversions.
No serious repeatable profit exploit was established by this audit.

DESIGN CONCERN: this anti-arbitrage policy is intentionally harsher than full
reimbursement. Expensive requests can reach the cap before their last delivery,
which then pays nothing. A player might reasonably prefer selling to a native
trader and resent a mandatory duty. Item-count tiers currently all share the
same stackable range, despite their names. Balance should account for effort,
not simply use a larger capped coin number.

KNOWN LIMITATION: a failed coin spawn consumes its saved reservation. This
prevents replay minting but can lose legitimate compensation. Separate world,
player-inventory and entity saves are not crash-atomic. Normal save/relog tests
do not simulate every process interruption between consumption and issuance.
Obsolete-request replacement preserves matching selected deposits, not every
old deposited resource or a universal refund. Do not promise lossless repair.

F. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE
======================================================================

State authority: KOMEPlayerProgression stores canonical rank; SerfKnight,
KnightServiceRecord and Lordship records own separate consecutive lifecycles.
There is no new parallel promotion state machine. Native offer shells do not
create native quest credit/rewards for KOME progression. Legacy achievements
remain a distinct permission/compatibility layer. This last overlap is a design
and maintenance concern rather than evidence that canonical rank is inferred
from arbitrary title strings.

Server/client boundary: direct NPC consumption occurs in server-side Forge
interaction handling before native GUI opening. Mutating packets retain the
existing server-thread wrapper. Public identities, tracker state, map markers
and Quest reasons are one-way projections; client timers/strings do not confer
rank. The new public roster carries only UUID/suffix, not private quest data.
The name transformer modifies only native world lettering after native range/
visibility checks. GameProfile, command names, chat and tab identity are not
rewritten. Only exact legacy KOME title-team memberships are retired.
Evidence: D/KOMEEvents.java:417; Q/KOMECommandKome.java:103;
N/KOMEPacketHandler.java; C/KOMEPlayerNameplate.java;
src/main/java/kome/core/KOMEPlayerNameplateTransformer.java:12.

Persistence: item keys/metadata, tokens/revisions, destinations, deposits,
reward reservations, forced-completion provenance, failed-NPC identity/context/
timestamps and bounded eight-destination history are saved. New tags are
additive inside existing records. Sixteen recent failure records are retained.
Ordinary faction changes use relationship lifecycle methods, retaining the
historical failed-NPC ledger rather than client timer authority. The new
20-minute wall-clock restriction is distinct from calendar-day cadence.

Entity ownership: required enemies and temporary charges use existing binding
and lease reconciliation. Incidental ambush mobs only carry an incidental tag;
they have no objective token, required-enemy outline or progression persistence
lease. They retain native despawn behavior. Escort cleanup restores the original
teleport flag; protected actors restore their native home lease. Unloaded roles
are reconciled on natural entity/item load, not force-loaded for convenience.

Rendering: old outline code reserved Forge stencil bits, which are normally zero
without a special display configuration. The new native-model hook draws a
front-culled enlarged red hull after ordinary model depth, with scoped attribute/
matrix state and restored active texture. It does not mutate framebuffers or
recursively render whole entities. Only loaded, natively rendered, owner-relevant
required NPCs at 32-96 blocks qualify. It does not glow through terrain and
does not include a separate armor-shell outline. Visual success is unverified.
Evidence: C/KOMEProgressionEnemyOutline.java:18,23;
src/main/java/kome/core/KOMEProgressionOutlineTransformer.java:12.

Network compatibility: existing IDs, retired holes and synchronized dev tactical
IDs 48-51 plus response 52 remain. Public identity appends 53. Eligibility payload
now also includes relevance/reason. Identical updated builds on client/server are
required; old-build network interoperability was not tested or promised. The
artifact version is still 1.0.8, so distribution must distinguish this build.

Commands: /kome progression finishDuty/status/why/resetDuty are behind native
staff permission before player/world access. Mutations enter existing completion/
cleanup routes, log to the central audit stream, preserve personal reporting and
do not auto-grant rank. Forced commission reports do not earn faction service
credit; forced trials/duties suppress earned announcements/rewards. Console
execution without a player is rejected. Existing admin rank/relationship/cadence
tools are separate explicit overrides and remain available.

Performance: new variety searches remain bounded; no new global history database
or block-generation infrastructure exists. The surrounding NPC reconciliation
and offer code still scans loaded NPCs and saved progression records frequently.
The public name roster builds an online-player roster during each player's title
refresh. These deserve TPS/network profiling under realistic population; a
small JVM fixture cannot certify scale. Compact one-line domain methods also
make audits and future maintenance harder than the passing suite suggests.

G. IN-GAME PLAUSIBILITY / ACCEPTANCE MATRIX
======================================================================

Every item below is LIVE-TESTING REQUIRED; none was live-tested in this pass.

Nameplates: verify actual font size, long faction titles, skins/nicknames,
scoreboard below-name objectives, sneaking, invisibility, line wrapping (native
nameplates generally do not wrap), relog and two observers after promotion.
The profile-preservation and world-only hook tests do not establish readability.

Outlines: test standard NPCs, armor, capes/transparent textures, riders, distant
entities, chunk/frustum limits, through-terrain absence, shaders/render mods and
NVIDIA drivers. Confirm a red silhouette rather than a subtle/unreadable rim.
Capture GL errors/frame rate before and after. No inference links the prior
NVIDIA crash conclusively to this code or establishes that this change fixes it.

Map: verify full zoom, correct center, moving escort markers, manual preference
restoration after close/resizing, highlight removal at approximately ten seconds
and persistent assignment marker. Also explicitly test return/report stages,
where the current target policy excludes relationship markers.

Escorts: fences, rivers, bridges, slopes, fleeing, owner death/logout, another
player loading the charge's chunk, wait/recalculate controls and a genuinely
reachable shelter. Arrival currently depends on actual NPC proximity, not
player-only travel. That is correct but does not make pathfinding reliable.

Ambushes: friendly chance is 6% versus 18% in other eligible territory at the
minute gate after at least 128 accumulated travel blocks. Maximum group is two,
nearby hostile/incidental threshold four, and existing nearby hostility blocks
additional special rolls. Record realized encounters per journey, not just the
configured probability. Native terrain/spawn checks may suppress most attempts.
Confirm ordinary despawning and zero bound combat credit/outline. Cumulative
travel does not require new forward progress before every later roll, so
camping after initial travel deserves observation.

Refuges and geography: measure actual successful assignment frequency in fresh
and existing worlds across factions. Physical observation has loaded-world,
distance and registry limits; a native settlement existing somewhere is not
proof that this request can find it. Incursions require native hostility across
the selected boundary, not invented territorial ownership.

Speech/handover: confirm the actual speaker, timely native speech, pouch sound
at the receiving NPC, pop only for deliberate progression drops, no duplicate
client/server sound, closed GUI on success and normal native interaction when
no immediate handover applies. Test negative cases and unrelated held items.

NPC nobility/rulers: sample real Lord/Prince encounter frequencies and capitals.
Prince is assigned to one quarter of new eligible hiring NPCs, despite the
RARE descriptor. Capitals must be loaded and safely placeable before a ruler
can physically appear. Singleton/incarnation tests do not certify every faction
capital or guarantee a recognizable royal residence.

Dedicated/integrated: same updated JAR on both sides; restart with real NBT,
death/chunk unload/faction change, lagged/replayed interaction packets, operators
and nonoperators, failed spawn hooks and changed mod/protection combinations.
The full suite validates common-side linkage, not an actual running dedicated
server. Do not certify production readiness from the build alone.

H. CONTRADICTIONS AND UNFINISHED DESIGN
======================================================================

Resolved in this working tree: the former Master no longer confers Knight;
gift collection is optional; courier HUD no longer pretends numeric progress;
recovery icons use actual saved contents; failed Standing relationships release;
new incidental ambushes are separate from required attackers.

Still present:
  1. Personal service is the canonical Knight/Lord route, while legacy
     advancement flags still unlock tools and content independently.
  2. Lordship tests command of others; Lord gameplay lacks the matching new
     personal-responsibility introduction and dedicated service HUD.
  3. Lord -> Prince is an achievement/completion-marker bridge, not implemented
     service to the NPC Prince. Its capture-five step can be self-attested.
  4. "Fell Beast" is a label for rolled Balrog or Ent/troll-chieftain achievements,
     and "Early Beginnings" says connect five builds to roads while its current
     automatic implementation checks represented faction population >= 200.
  5. Generic recovery registry prose mentions NPCs/caravans/livestock, but the
     implemented Standing recovery is one bound lost item.
  6. General escort danger is incidental, but the existing dangerous Knight
     escort still requires its bound encounter's threatResolved flag before
     arrival completes. Escaping its mandatory attackers cannot finish that
     commission, even though escaping new incidental mobs can.
  7. Availability reasoning and passive native offer readiness are separate;
     some unavailable Quest-button cases still lack the promised reason.
  8. Native playable factions and KOME supported Liege definitions are not
     identical: Fangorn has no legitimate native host for this service path.

These are not proposed new implementation requirements secretly carried out
during the audit. They are grounded issues for subsequent design/coding review.
Legacy rank-quota/standing rows are filtered from the Advancements tab; it
would be inaccurate to say all old rank checklists are simultaneously displayed
there. Their definitions, commands, offering inventory and unlock consequences
still exist and need deliberate disposition.

J. OVERALL ASSESSMENT AND NEXT FIVE IMPROVEMENTS
======================================================================

Scale: 1 = absent/incoherent or a major unresolved barrier; 2 = substantial
gaps; 3 = usable foundation with noticeable friction; 4 = coherent by code and
strong tests with bounded unresolved concerns; 5 = coherent and demonstrated
in sustained live play. No dimension is awarded 5 without live evidence.

Progression clarity: 3/5. The path into Knight and through Lordship is readable
in current objectives, but secondary unlocks, real-day cadence and Lord's
arrival still require interpretation. Live onboarding remains necessary.

Rank/relationship coherence: 4/5. Current Liege owns Knight/Lord appointments,
Master retirement is explicit and gifts are optional. Exact superior policy
and political King independence are strong. The Prince transition remains a
different, incomplete appointment philosophy.

LOTR authenticity: 3/5. Native factions, item stacks, occupation signals,
enemy geography and speech are substantial integration. Common water fallback,
shared gift language and universal late checklists reduce cultural specificity.

Quest variety: 3/5. Three Master duties, three Standing trials, five commission
types and three Lordship scenarios exist; bounded history adds useful variation.
Several services still share the same travel/combat skeleton. Live repetition
and destination feasibility must be sampled before adding more nominal types.

NPC believability: 3/5. Role-based requests and actual-speaker warnings/thanks
help; public earned standing gives service social consequence. Not every
occupation/culture has distinctive dialogue or a meaningful higher-rank role.

World integration: 3/5. No fictional refuge construction or invented borders;
real actors and native relationships matter. Shelter observation/reachability,
NPC noble density and ruler placement need actual-world validation.

UI consistency: 3/5. Concise courier HUD, real icons, current-service filtering
and max zoom are improvements. Relationship-only navigation and passive-offer
reason gaps remain; Lord HUD is absent. No visual readability claim is made.

Failure recovery: 3/5. Failed Standing relationships release; cooldowns and
history persist; lost-item attempts and leases are bounded and reconciled.
Technical activation penalties, deposit replacement and failed reward issuance
still create avoidable losses/frustration.

Technical reliability: 4/5. The complete suite and normal build pass; canonical
authority, binding, replay guards, additive NBT and preserved packet IDs are
good foundations. Real crash/save ordering, dedicated multiplayer, rendering
mods and performance are not certified by those tests.

Readiness for further Lord/Prince development: 2/5. The temporary-command trial
provides a good starting point, but the Lord landing experience, authority
content and legacy permission/checklist disposition should be decided first.
Adding Prince features immediately would amplify the current discontinuity.

The five most important next improvements for a coherent Wanderer -> Lord
experience, in priority order:
  1. Resolve/publish native-faction support boundaries, especially Fangorn,
     before a player invests in an allegiance without a viable service path.
  2. Design the Lord arrival and first responsibility, explicitly introducing
     Prince authority and the existing strategic powers instead of a cliff.
  3. Reconcile legacy permission activities with earned standing and make the
     optional-training versus mandatory-promotion distinction unmistakable.
  4. Preflight trial actors/geography and distinguish technical issuance failure
     from a failed undertaking that deserves rejection and real-day penalties.
  5. Complete a recorded live acceptance playthrough: real names, outlines,
     dialogue/handover, maps, escort paths/ambushes and payout effort. Tune from
     observed player behavior and failures, not source presence or test totals.

Preserve what already works: canonical rank separate from political office;
native pledge/faction authority; UUID-based service relationships; Liege-owned
appointments; optional gift pickup; current-stage objectives; actual NPC arrival;
no escort teleport; conservative real-world destinations; bound property tokens/
revisions; required-versus-incidental enemy ownership; temporary-command
survival responsibility; server-side consumption/reservations; saved daily
cadence and failure context; and the integrated dev strategic/conflict systems.

Final assessment: the personal-service journey to Lord is now substantially
more coherent in its authority and interactions. It is a tested code foundation,
not a completed live-validated progression experience or finished Lord/Prince
design. The next pass should resolve the supported-path and Lord-handoff
decisions while measuring the actual Minecraft experience.
