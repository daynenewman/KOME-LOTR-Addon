# Progression feedback backlog

Prepared 2026-10-17 against stabilization `be12f0f` plus inherited language recovery.
This is a proposed implementation order, not approval to change mechanics.
First run [the current acceptance session](../testing/PLAYTEST_LATEST.md) and repair
any reliability regressions. Keep promotion, recommendation, military and native
alignment authority in their owning services.

| Order | Feedback / proposed slice | Dependencies and balancing decisions | Verification before release |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Consistent rank-aware developer relationship commands | Existing `KOMESerfKnightRelationshipService.ForceLevel` already covers Serf/Knight/Lord. Audit target rank, current service cleanup, role leases, recommendation context and forced-credit isolation before adding command variants. Commands must not turn exercise work into earned promotion. | Matrix of player/target ranks, invalid roles, active-duty cleanup, save/reload and exercise isolation. |
| 2 | More varied faction food/material requests | Extend existing provisioning/profession catalogs with native faction supplies, availability and trade eligibility. Agree request value, travel burden and scarcity budgets; retain fallback when goods cannot be prepared. Avoid increasing cost through variety alone. | Representative Orc/Uruk/Elf/Dorwinion catalogs, obtainable item/damage/vessel combinations, partial hand-in and reimbursement. |
| 3 | Better dialogue and courier letters | Build on `KOMEProgressionNpcSpeech` and courier correspondence. Use faction/occupation and objective stage, short instructions, native names and correct issuer/recipient. Preserve IDs and serialized evidence. | Long names, generated letters, current stages, repeat interactions and book fit. |
| 4 | Master's coins visibly dropping into the world | Physical coin drops already exist in `KOMEProgressionServiceRewards` through `KOMEProgressionItemDrops`, generally at the player. First reproduce the reported missing effect. Decide whether presentation should move to the Master and define recovery if the world rejects issuance. Retain one saved payment reservation; do not add a second reward path. | Actual Minecraft visibility/pickup, full inventory, rejected spawn, relog/replay and duplicate payment protection. |
| 5 | Greater commission distance/variety; avoid repeated locations and protected NPCs | Extend the existing eight-destination history and type weighting in `KOMEProgressionVariety`, plus authoritative role/encounter exclusions. Native territory, loaded-site validation and genuine destination proof remain gates. Choose distance bands by terrain and transport access; avoid travel time becoming the main difficulty. | Repeated journeys across factions/terrain, small feasible pools, fallback exhaustion, protected role leases and unloaded sites. |
| 6 | Consume recommendation letters on redemption | Current saved recommendation provenance is single-use; the item remains a keepsake. Decide whether all authentic copies or only the presented copy disappear, and what a lost/rejected transfer does. Saved redemption must remain the authority even if an item survives a crash. | Copied/forged/stale books, full inventory, repeated packets and crash/reload around redemption. |
| 7 | Armed/armored attackers | Choose faction-appropriate native equipment through existing encounter actors. Decide loot, alignment/XP and difficulty changes before equipping. Preserve staff actors' no-reward rules and protected civilians. | Faction equipment, combat damage, actor save/cleanup, loot and exercise isolation. |
| 8 | Harder Knight encounters, potentially 6-8 enemies | Depends on equipment and reliable followers. Use commission tier and party size; define spawn density, simultaneous attackers, reinforcements and escape conditions. Six to eight is a tuning proposal, not a new default. | Solo lower-tier survivability, geared higher tiers, cramped terrain, multiplayer scaling and protected patrol stability. |
| 9 | Meaningful settlement attacks and more Knight mission types | Build objective-specific stakes on existing defense/rescue/help/relief/infiltration stages. Design civilian/building loss and rollback policy before introducing persistent harm. Prefer non-destructive objectives initially. Avoid another travel-and-kill reskin. | Distinct success/failure evidence, approachable destinations, interrupted encounters, reload and no unapproved world damage. |
| 10 | Liege alignment rewards instead of coins | Existing commission reward selection varies by mission. Define native faction alignment amount/caps and whether it contributes toward the 2,000 promotion threshold. Prevent reward farming and staff exercise credit. | One-time rewards, faction switches, death/reload, promotion pacing and all commission types. |
| 11 | Two soldiers as a departing Liege gift | Decide which transition qualifies, whether soldiers are temporary followers or permanent native hires, ownership/population/maintenance cost, equipment and death behavior. Coordinate progression with hiring/companies and native persistence; do not copy temporary patrol actors into permanent service. | One gift per eligible transition, inventory/world spawn failure, owner handoff, reload and legitimate military accounting. |
| 12 | Cooldown for abandoning/joining Master or Liege service | Product rule remains undefined. Decide voluntary abandonment vs death, faction change, technical failure and staff repair; duration/clock, scope and saved migration. Existing betrayal/failed legacy Liege restrictions are separate rules. Stabilization cancels unfinished Master duties and preserves history without adding this cooldown. | After approval: explicit exemptions, restart/time changes, switching factions, ordinary death and prevention of permanent lockout. |

Dependencies suggest small independently accepted slices through orders 1-6, then
equipment before encounter counts, followed by new objectives and reward economy.
Soldier gifts require the military ownership design. The cooldown can be designed
separately but must not be inferred from a reliability defect. No backlog item is
included as an expected passing test in the current Minecraft checklist.
