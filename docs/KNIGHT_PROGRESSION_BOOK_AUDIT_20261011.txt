KNIGHT PROGRESSION BOOK UX AUDIT — 2026-10-11

Scope and evidence
------------------
This is a source and automated-test audit of the existing progression branch.
It is not a screenshot review or Minecraft acceptance test. No live client was
launched for this pass. The native LOTR book textures, buttons, font, rank names,
scrolling and map navigation remain the presentation framework.

1. Original presentation problems
---------------------------------
The Ranks page put advancement requirements ahead of current work. A Knight
opening the general book could initially see the Advancements tab, while opening
the duty shortcut selected Ranks and scrolled to the bottom. The resulting
experience depended on the entry point and previous tab preference.

Knight service was described through several overlapping server projections:
KOMEKnightCommissionPresentation.summary, KOMEProgressionSummary and
KOMEProgressionRankSummary. The rank projection assigned Knight activity text
twice. Its summary mixed Liege identity, credits, preparation instructions,
historical descriptions and the active commission. The historical information
sent in canonicalSummary was not consistently useful in the rank-page layout.

An inactive Lordship trial could be used as the activity heading while the
player was still completing commissions. Report-ready commissions retained
destination and combat explanations. Relief inventory enrichment could replace
the current-stage context and continue showing supply instructions after the
field task was complete. A report-stage tracker showed 0/1 even though the
action was a conversation. Binary rank requirements also occupied space with
1/1 or 0/1 labels.

The old Lordship narrative directed the player to the existing Lord Liege and
required returning surviving soldiers. Both conflicted with the requested
Prince-issued invasion and personal-survival rule. The general commission
summary repeated a preparation line even when there was current field work.

Legacy rank-quota rows were already filtered out of the Advancements view by
KOMEProgressionAchievement.forAdvancementsGroup. This pass does not claim to
have removed a legacy checklist that was still visible there. Saved legacy
achievement and quota data remain compatible.

--------------------------
KOMEGuiProgression:
  constructor, drawScreen, selectView, drawRanks, drawKnightRanks,
  drawRankRequirement, rankContentHeight and refreshViewButtons.
KOMEProgressionRankSummary.project:
  server-authored current rank, real requirements and active activity.
KOMEKnightBookPresentation.context:
  relationship and compact credited-service footer.
KOMEProgressionSummary.text (alignment-aware overload):
  Knight context supplied to the native book, separate from current activity.
KOMEKnightCommissionPresentation:
  objective, stageLabel and details.
KOMELordshipTrialPresentation:
  native invasion orders, current stage, Prince guidance and report narrative.
KOMEProgressionGoodsPresentation.objective/enrich:
  inventory-aware relief detail, restricted to an active field delivery.
KOMEProgressionTrackerSnapshot.commission/lordship:
  binary versus quantitative objective presentation.
KOMEVisualLocationService and KOMEProgressionMapFocus:
  authoritative objective identity and native Show Me target selection.
KOMEPacketProgressionData and KOMEPacketVisualMarkers:
  existing server-to-client projection boundaries.

3. Improvements implemented
---------------------------
Knights receive a Service & Rank tab within the existing book. The normal book
and duty shortcut both begin with current service, including when the first
snapshot arrives after the GUI opens. Explicit selection of Advancements is
still respected. Serf and higher-rank page layouts retain their existing flow.

The Knight page now reads in this order:
  Current service: only the active commission/trial, or a concise next action.
  Standing: distinct faction-attributed service credits, current alignment and
            the actual Lordship field/report requirement.
  Relationship: current Lord Liege and separate Prince trial authority, using
                the existing faction-specific native authority titles.
  Credited service: one compact entry per credited commission type.

Travel, battle, recovery, delivery, report and technical preparation have
different text. Report-ready work is a short return instruction; it does not
repeat the full encounter description. Relief supplies are not presented as
current work after delivery. The book preserves the marked recipient and
subsequent report instruction when showing inventory-aware relief quantities.

Eligibility directs a qualified Knight toward a Prince, explains that the
service/alignment requirements are fulfilled, and describes the actual invasion
trial without pretending it is already active. Cultural authority definitions
provide general native spawn-host guidance. An exact Prince map target requires
a personally encountered saved identity or the actual issuing authority.
Unencountered loaded Princes are not automatically revealed.

Show Me follows the saved settlement/attack anchor during travel/battle and the
correct Lord or Prince during reporting. If no Prince is known, the button has
no fictional destination. The book provides general discovery guidance instead.
Technical preparation text explains retry/reassignment; it does not blame the
player for unloaded chunks or a failed spawn transaction.

Binary tracker objectives have no fraction or bar. Goods, combat, service-count
and alignment requirements retain real quantities. Binary book requirement rows
keep the native completion indicator and omit the redundant numerical quota.
Provision markers use native bread; profession markers use the requested native
material stack, including metadata. This applies to tracker, map and NPC icon
paths while retaining existing indicator priority.

4. Remaining limitations
------------------------
The narrow native page still needs scrolling for long invasion orders and
cultural discovery guidance. There is no new pagination system. The compact
history shows credited types for the current faction, not a complete chronology
of every attempt or service for former factions. Full historical evidence is
still persisted by the service record.

Known Prince locations are last observed locations and can become stale while
unloaded. Loaded movement/rank/death evidence refreshes or removes guidance.
The book has no omniscient global noble search. Native noble availability can
still make the discovery step difficult, particularly in explored old worlds
whose existing noble records were migrated as Lords.

New explanatory strings are English, with native item/faction/title naming.
They have not received a complete localization or typography review. The
remaining higher-rank legacy progression pages are outside this pass.

5. Contradictory text and state
------------------------------
The live Knight activity/report paths no longer instruct players to keep two
soldiers alive or have a Lord issue a new Lordship trial. Compatibility language
keys for survivor/report messages were corrected as well.

Saved version-1 trials retain their original field objectives and are labelled
as saved trials. Their original Lord remains the provenance of those field
orders; a Prince receives the final report and grants Lord standing. Such a
trial may still protect a civilian or deliver a legacy charge. This is explicit
continuation, not a claim that an old generic encounter was a native invasion.

The compatibility three-argument commission summary remains available for
non-book callers. The actual Knight book uses the alignment-aware context and
rank activity projection. A future consolidation could retire that duplicate
summary path after auditing its command/admin consumers.

6. Recommended further improvements
-----------------------------------
Use live screenshots at common GUI scales to decide whether the cultural
guidance needs a short secondary page. Measure actual journey times in several
factions before tuning geography again. Consider a compact optional service
chronology and a clearly labelled last-seen timestamp for known authorities.
Complete localization after the live wording/layout has been accepted.

7. Required live visual tests
----------------------------
Open the normal book and duty shortcut as a Knight, including after reconnect.
Check travel, active combat, stolen-item recovery, relief delivery, ready report,
failed gameplay attempt, technical preparation and qualified idle states.
Verify the correct Lord/Prince title, no duplicated inactive assignment text,
no clipped long titles, scrollbar extent and footer/button spacing.
Verify Show Me during travel, combat and reporting; test an unknown Prince,
a known moving Prince, an unloaded Prince and a dead/replacement authority.
Check bread and profession icons on HUD, map and NPC head without overlaps.
Repeat at multiple GUI scales and with a multiplayer client/server pair.
