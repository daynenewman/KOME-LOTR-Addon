KOME-LOTR-ADDON: PROGRESSION, RANK ABILITIES AND LEGACY CLEANUP AUDIT
Audit date: 2026-10-12
AUDIT ONLY. NO PROPOSED GAMEPLAY CHANGES HAVE BEEN IMPLEMENTED.

A. EXECUTIVE ASSESSMENT
=======================

The permanent standing model is sound; the ability model is not yet coherent.
The actual player ladder is Wanderer -> Serf -> Knight -> Lord -> Prince. King
is stored separately as a political office. Service through Knight and Lord
promotion now uses real saved NPC relationships, objectives and personal reports.
These are substantial improvements over the old checkbox progression. However,
automatic privileges still mostly come from legacy achievement markers and
optional/self-attested advancements, rather than the canonical rank. The new
branch metadata does not yet replace the legacy catalog.

The strongest recommendation is to retain the existing canonical rank, extend
the existing permission registry into a small derived ability policy, and retain
domain services as the authorities for troop control, appointments, resources,
native eligibility and political actions. Do not build a second player-ability
save ledger or a new RPG framework.

The highest-priority problems are:
1. A legacy Knight marker enables ordinary and campaign combat hiring against
   the faction bank. There is no Crown allocation or Lord 200-population ceiling.
2. Lord capture permission can cause a hostile claim to create a war and worsen
   diplomacy, without recognized ruler authorization or a battle-victory check
   in the inspected player-claim path. Sovereign consequences are not separated
   from territorial participation.
3. Basic survival remains gated by cooking, fire, farming, stone tools, meat and
   mounts. Rank promotion does not reliably resolve all of those restrictions.
4. The client progression book receives a server rank projection, while ordinary
   permission reads use another client progression map that this packet does
   not update. The visible book and waypoint eligibility can disagree.
5. Lord -> Prince still uses the old alignment/capture/boss/butterfly checklist
   and a self-completion command. No fief appointment or Crown population budget
   exists to turn the advertised higher standing into a cohesive responsibility.

The most dangerous LEGACY PERMISSION conflict is baseline.hire_units: it is
derived from serf.title_knight and is read without a canonical Lord-rank check.
It permits strategic hiring at Knight and can survive a stale marker or an
inconsistent rank record. Because recruitment spends the common faction bank,
this affects other players, not merely the owner's progression. The hostile
claim/war coupling is an additional CRITICAL authority problem.

B. CURRENT PROGRESSION ARCHITECTURE
==================================

Server state and mutation:
- KOMEWorldData is server WorldSavedData (KOME_ServerRules); progressions is keyed
  by player UUID. Native LOTRPlayerData separately owns pledge, alignment, native
  achievements, quest totals and fast-travel state. Character PlayerRaceData
  owns race/creation and original starting waypoint. NPC and item NBT own physical
  identities, role/encounter tokens and native follower state.
- KOMEPlayerProgression owns CanonicalRank plus legacy Completed, Assignments,
  QuotaDelivered, 54 Offering slots, old PledgedLord fields, SerfKnightProgression,
  KnightService, Lordship and queued relationship death notices.
- KOMECanonicalRankService is the explicit rank mutation boundary. enterSerfdom
  requires a saved Master; ordinary pledge alone never grants Serf. Staff override
  cancels encounters and resets incompatible Serf state, reconciles legacy rank
  markers and recomputes flags. It must remain distinct from earned promotion.
- Serf duties/trial authority is KOMESerfKnightService plus world-facing Master,
  provisioning, profession, courier, escort, recovery and defense services.
- KnightServiceRecord stores reported, faction-provenance service history. Three
  DISTINCT non-admin-forced service types for the CURRENT faction plus +2,000
  alignment qualify a Knight to seek a Prince for Lordship.
- KOMELordshipTrialService issues/reports the trial; current new trials bind a
  native invasion with a temporary force. The successful Prince report alone
  promotes Knight -> Lord in ordinary gameplay.
- KOMEHigherRankTransitionService now rejects the old Knight -> Lord recipe but
  still handles Lord -> Prince using legacy requirements. Registry title markers
  are retained for compatibility.
- KOMERulerService stores one recognized player UUID/name per faction, independent
  of rank. KOMEProgressionRulerService separately owns persistent NPC King identity,
  incarnation, death/respawn and capital placement. An NPC King is not a player
  budget/declaration service.

Permission/projection architecture:
- KOMEProgressionPermissions.has reads achievement completion/default flags.
  Trading and pouches return true for the compatibility IDs. Most other IDs do
  not consult rank. Capture/reclaim additionally require current Lord or above.
  Disabled progression bypasses these legacy permission checks; strategic,
  political and native restrictions remain separate.
- KOMEProgressionPermissionRegistry is a static inventory/prerequisite policy.
  Gate entries are text/status metadata; gates() is not an authoritative action
  dispatcher. canComplete constrains old GROUP completion to a canonical rank.
- KOMEProgressionAutoCompleter maps native achievement/other evidence to old
  completions, then stores derived baseline flags. applyUnlocks adds flags;
  recomputeUnlocks revokes/regrants derived flags. Neither is a new ability system.
- KOMEProgressionBranch lists exploration_adventure, craft_lore,
  husbandry_farming, warfare_siegecraft and rohan_horsemanship. The 96 installed
  legacy definitions have null canonical branch/rank/completion/reward metadata.
  canonical(...) validates a possible definition but does not register it in ALL
  or BY_ID. Warfare branch unlock and Prince capstones are not functional content.
- Server summaries feed packet/UI, while the NPC offer bridge injects native quest
  shells and narrow transformers hook native interaction, naming, waypoint,
  property processing and invasion completion. Services must validate requests
  again; native quest shell display is not objective completion authority.

F-001 | HIGH | Canonical rank versus derived permission history
Evidence: VERIFIED IN SOURCE.
Actual: has() mostly trusts Completed; applyUnlocks derives normal privileges
from older advancement/title IDs. Promotion and ability availability use different
authorities. Capture is the notable current-rank exception.
Source: src/main/java/kome/common/data/KOMEProgressionPermissions.java:34-54,
has; KOMEProgressionAutoCompleter.java:118-165, applyUnlocks/recomputeUnlocks;
KOMECanonicalRankService.java, overrideCanonicalRank/enterSerfdom.
Consequence: an earned rank can lack expected abilities; a stale historical marker
can retain current powers. Admin repair can change gear access through unrelated
flags, and future additions risk extending contradictory authorities.
Recommendation: derive permanent ability eligibility from current rank; retain
achievement completions as history/expertise only. Do not persist rank-derived
ability flags as a second authority.
Immediate cleanup: partial; inventory/text cleanup is safe, behavioral migration
needs scenario coverage and explicit handling of existing users.
Decision: only the unresolved exceptional/specialist policy, not the supplied ladder.
Live testing: yes, rank change/relog/admin/faction-switch and action paths.

F-002 | MEDIUM | Branch registry is scaffolding, not specialization gameplay
Evidence: VERIFIED IN SOURCE; NOT YET IMPLEMENTED for the target branches.
Actual: installed entries use the legacy constructor; canonical factory does not
install definitions, and GUI still selects six legacy groups.
Source: KOMEProgressionAchievement.java:29-60 and static catalog;
KOMEProgressionBranch.java, static registration; client/gui/KOMEGuiProgression.java:28.
Consequence: a named branch or metadata test can be mistaken for a playable
Warfare specialty or Prince capstone.
Recommendation: keep the small registry, but first design concrete specialist
rewards and production operations. Mark the unused branches as planned in UI/docs.
Immediate cleanup: yes, accurate descriptions; no speculative branch content.
Decision: yes, specialist content/rewards.
Live testing: required when real actions are connected.

  normal faction military equipment use/wear -> MIGRATE TO RANK where KOME gate
  actually exists; possession/receipt/storage remain unrestricted as now.
  specialized manufacturing -> RETAIN AS SPECIALIZATION only for designed recipes.
  Warfare branch access -> MIGRATE TO RANK; content/rewards still need design.
  company/battle participation -> MIGRATE TO RANK + exact faction/operation authority.
  no personal strategic recruitment/army ownership merely from Knight rank.
  serves a valid same-faction Lord; participation is not personal company command.
Lord:
  strategic recruit/create/company movement/command eligibility -> MIGRATE TO RANK.
  actual spending -> APPOINTMENT/RESOURCE-GATED: valid Crown allocation, coins,
  native recruit eligibility, location, campaign governance and server safety caps.
  owned living strategic troop population <= 200; NOT a 200-population gift.
  siege gate operation -> MIGRATE TO RANK only for applicable military gates,
  retaining gate ownership/access and hostile/siege state. Personal neutral doors
  or civilian gate access must not accidentally become Lord-only.
  expanded holdings/workers -> only real implemented managed Build/worker actions;
  do not label a nonexistent estate framework as unlocked.
  military operations -> appointment/orders/active war; no independent war declaration.
  serves a Prince.
Prince:
  inherits Lord ability eligibility; no PERSONAL RANK ceiling, but still budget,
  global safety limits, valid native units/location and political jurisdiction.
  fief control -> APPOINTMENT/RESOURCE-GATED, requires a real assignment/territory
  record (NOT YET IMPLEMENTED); rank alone is insufficient.
  capstone eligibility -> MIGRATE TO RANK; actual specialist rewards not yet designed.
  fealty -> appropriate recognized sovereign authority.
King office:
  Prince eligibility + recognized office; grants sovereign diplomatic/declaration
  and delegation operations without adding a sixth permanent rank.
  implemented diplomacy can be reused; player declaration and population delegation
  require authoritative production boundaries. No succession implementation here.

Do not choose higher-rank waypoint expansion, kingless population delegation or
fief qualification in this matrix. See P. Native ordinary combat hiring also
needs an explicit scope decision: the prompt prohibits Knight strategic army
recruitment, but does not settle every ordinary personal LOTR bodyguard case.

G. RECOMMENDED AUTHORITATIVE ABILITY ARCHITECTURE
===============================================

Use the smallest reasonable extension of KOMEProgressionPermissionRegistry and
KOMEProgressionPermissions, keeping KOMECanonicalRankService and existing domain
Decision/result services. Rename the registry only if that improves clarity; a
new sprawling ability subsystem is unnecessary.

Suggested minimal representation (DESIGN RECOMMENDATION):
Ability definition: stable id, minimum permanent rank OR sovereign office,
optional designed expertise key, localization/icon keys, declared applicability.
Ability eligibility result: unlocked, blocker kind, requirement key/text. Do not
persist "unlocked" when rank/expertise already determines it.
Action context remains owned by the relevant service: faction, target/company/
gate/Build/waypoint, appointment authority, live native eligibility, resource
budget and operation state. Do not load or scan the world to build a rank card.

Concrete possible methods:
- KOMEProgressionPermissions.unlocked(progression, abilityId): pure rank/expertise
  eligibility, no effects/no record creation. Higher rank inherits using order.
- ...eligibility(data, playerId, faction, abilityId): office/faction-aware view.
- RecruitmentService.evaluateRecruitment(context): composes eligibility, live
  native rules, allocated spend, personal ceiling, safety limits and location.
- CompanyCommandAuthority.evaluateCommand(context): composes standing with exact
  ownership/delegation/governance and current operational limitations.
- WaypointAccessService.evaluatePlayer continues to own destination/native policy.
- The service returns an actual blocker (RANK / EXPERTISE / APPOINTMENT / FACTION /
  ALIGNMENT / RESOURCE / NATIVE / OPERATIONAL / TECHNICAL), plus relevant numbers.
  Reuse existing immutable Decision/result objects rather than introducing a
  general expression language or generic authorization engine.

Inherited abilities are a computed >= rank rule, not a separate granted set.
If a future specialty explicitly demands rank, it is rank + expertise; if an
ordinary ability is already unlocked by rank, old specialty completion must not
be an additional condition. Faction eligibility may change on pledge switching;
history does not. King office composes with Prince-level eligibility only under
the chosen eligibility rule; it does not mutate permanent rank during rendering,
login, repair or migration. An ineligible legacy King record needs a reviewable
compatibility/admin resolution, not a hidden automatic promotion.

Appointments confer current jurisdiction: controlling THIS company/gate/holding/
territory, not every asset of that class. Resource delegations confer spending
authority, not a different permanent rank. A Lord with zero allocated population
still has recruitment unlocked; the action result says "No delegated population".
Safety caps remain another action blocker and apply equally to Prince/King.

Do not implement by maintaining completed rank-derived baseline flags forever.
First change permission READS for migrated abilities, preserving saved history.
Retire old applyUnlocks writes only after every consumer is mapped. Provide a
temporary legacy-ID alias to the derived rule, not a second conflicting authority.
Once native/ordinary retired IDs no longer control an operation, their records
may remain solely for history; no repeated migration is needed to maintain access.

Packet projection: add stable canonical rank key rather than inferring rank from
localized cultural title strings. Send viewer-specific office/expertise and a
small ability eligibility list/bitset if useful; action menus ask their own services
for current contextual availability. No duplicated persistent client progression.
Advance the matching-artifact protocol consistently if wire fields change.

Admin rules: keep setrank/reset/grant and office appointment explicit and audited.
No earned effects on override/recompute/relog. Progression-disabled policy needs
an explicit documented bypass (rank gates versus offices/security). Safe retreat,
return/egress/recovery must remain available when rank/faction access changes,
even if initiating a new operation is denied.

Enforcement boundary: commands and packets submit intents into the same domain
mutation service. Events should call that service/policy for native operations
that cannot use a KOME intent. Request + final teleport revalidation is correct;
duplicate handwritten rule copies in commands/UI are not. Put operation conditions
at commit as well as in preview, preserving server-thread scheduling and current
state revision/token checks.

J. SERF/KNIGHT GAMEPLAY COHERENCE AND CLEANUP FINDINGS
====================================================

Duty variety and occupation relevance
-------------------------------------
Provisioning and Profession are both gather several item types and hand them to
the same Master. They have different cultural/occupation pools and saved value,
but not inherently different decisions or world interactions. Relief is another
goods list at a remote beneficiary followed by Liege report. Courier messages/
parcels have different bound contents but the same travel/delivery/report loop.
Settlement defense and border incursion share native weighted enemy spawning,
participation and defeat/report mechanics; border ownership/enemy/geography and
protected settlement resident make the actual distinctions. Do not advertise five
completely distinct combat experiences based on enum names alone.

Catalog/acquisition constraints: profession pool includes occupation-specific
materials, but KOMEProgressionGoodsEligibility.profession filters to a small
registry allow-list and damage=0. Many advertised cultural items (regional alloy,
pearls, rare native ritual materials, saddle/fishing rod etc.) are removed by that
final filter. This protects attainability but reduces occupational variety. New
requests are 3-4 distinct types; quantity is stackLimit<=1 ? 1..16 : 32..64,
regardless of named QuantityTier. Material labels imply rarity tiers that current
quantity() ignores. Do not reintroduce unattainable alloy/military crafting demands
while fixing the labels. Existing nonzero metadata/saved requests must keep their
exact accepted policy; broadening a new pool is not a saved requirement rewrite.

Provision drinks: catalogs name mead, wines, juices, miruvor and native draughts,
then provision eligibility accepts ItemFood or exactly mugWater. Most ordinary
LOTR drinks are LOTRItemMug, not ItemFood, so those cultural candidates are filtered
and water fallback dominates. New vessel choices are singleton MUG. Detailed
metadata/strength rendering supports old exact requests, but current generation
does not mean all displayed vessel/strength varieties are produced. Saved drink
matching/presentation correctly distinguishes native item contents, vessel and
Weak/Light/Moderate/Strong/Potent, and legacy "any" requirements must remain broad.

Valid acquisition: new provision filters player cooking access for smelted food;
this avoids some impossible requests while the artificial cooking gate remains.
Removing ordinary survival gates simplifies candidate eligibility rather than
adding a new acquisition engine. Native coins/trade/local foods and ordinary mug
water are useful accessible sources. Do not count crafting near a trader based
on text alone; there is currently no craft proof for legacy Smithery/Craftsman.

Compensation: GoodsValue takes quarter of cheapest unmodified native pool unit
price, clamps 0..1000 milli-silver, fallback=10 (common) or 100 (ingots/copper/tin),
floor(total/1000), max payment=128. Partial deliveries use cumulative materialPaid;
reservation is saved before physical coins spawn. At 10 milli, 32..64 units of
one common type are worth zero whole silver alone; combined requests can produce
small payment. Unknown prices do not reflect gathering time/difficulty. Native
discount assumptions are conservative estimates, not proven economy telemetry.
Other duties/stolen goods give 2 silver; most commissions/trial use 2 food units.
Material return need not be lucrative, but repeatedly demanding 3-4 stacks for a
few coins may feel ungrateful. Costs/effort/reward need actual play observations.

Courier and escort
-------------------
Courier has saved correspondent, token, physical message/parcel, dispatch/resend
and delivery state, death/loss recovery and return-to-Master reporting. Source:
KOMESerfCourierAssignment (Version 8, 500..1500 blocks), KOMECourierService,
KOMECourierRecipientSpawner, issuance/recovery services. It is not an arbitrary
walk-distance self-report. Keep dispatch ownership and no-duplicate contents.

Standing recovery is a real native/vanilla bound lost item, 112..176 blocks away,
not the catalog's broad "missing NPC/patrol/caravan/livestock" promise. No hostile
thief is generated for Serf recovery. Native item/revision and use/processing
guards must survive cleanup. Knight stolen goods adds an actual enemy carrier
and one bound persistent drop: armor is equipped in its correct native slot;
other property uses idle/hand presentation while combat can switch weapons.

KOMEKnightCommissionService.displayProperty/equipCarrier/dropCarrierProperty;
KOMEQuestPropertyUseGuard. Do not recreate sealed paper wrappers for a native cap.

Escort now uses native following and a verified observed shelter/arrival proof:
traveler within 6 horizontal/2 vertical blocks of saved safe arrival, clear
approach, owner nearby (16-block participation in escort mechanics), living/bound

Combat fairness and technical failure
-------------------------------------
Knight enemies are 5..10 validated native weighted invasion entries, with mount,
boss/unsafe heavy exclusions and limited elite mixing. Settlement encounter
activation waits for arrival/preparation; spawn sites are safe-ground perimeter
search (24..40 blocks) around recorded shelter, not a complete native fort survey.
Pre-engagement resident loss can be technical retry rather than blame. Resident
protection does not equal complete settlement defense reliability; real NPC AI,
terrain and unrelated hostiles can still matter.

Mandatory personal enemies have server marker-based red silhouette highlighting
within 96 blocks, including native invasion actors. Incidental/unrelated enemies
are excluded. Native model rendering/depth state has dedicated tests but no new
live GPU verification here. The effect changes information/stealth expectations;
do not extend it to generic hostile territory or other players' objectives.

Technical Standing activation failure preserves trial/story and refunds its daily
slot with five-second retry. Genuine failure retains daily limit and same-Liege
20-minute failure memory. Knight/Lordship technical activation uses 200 world-tick
retry and report-based technical reassignment, not identical Standing behavior.
Missing loaded evidence is counted rather than missing entities treated as dead;
one-minute loaded-controller absence can retry native invasion. Multiple recovery
systems should converge on clear technical/gameplay failure vocabulary, not
discard every differing lifecycle rule.

Resource accounting must stay independent from standing
-------------------------------------------------------
Current cost calculator: farmhand=0; configured override>=1 otherwise max health
rounded by caller, mounted surcharge=25. Example: a 20-health unmounted unit costs
20 population; a same-health mounted unit costs 45. The proposed Lord limit is
population, not 200 entity units. A 200 budget/ceiling would fit ten such ordinary
unmounted troops or four mounted ones with 20 remaining, if that current cost
basis is retained. Overrides/level health make these examples conditional.

Faction available bank uses centi-population. A combat hire permanently invests
populationSpent; cost increases debit only above high-water investment, reductions
do not refund, death/dismissal do not normally refund. Active/represented population
projection uses saved living unit records, including unloaded/virtual movement.
Company investment sums CAMPAIGN member records. A missing entity is not dead.

Minimal target needs independent answers to:
  unlocked recruit standing?
  legitimate faction appointment/budget allowing THIS actor to spend?
  enough native coins and native unit eligibility?
  enough remaining Crown-allocated spending authorization AND faction bank?
  enough personal living strategic population room (Lord <=200)?
  server entity/company/operation safety allowance?
  legal/safe location and campaign/governance state?
All must pass before committing native effects. Promotion alone passes only first.

Recommended candidate basis for personal ceiling: saved living CAMPAIGN combat
records owned by player (not temporary controller), using one agreed cost basis,
including moving/unloaded/mounted units. Choosing investment versus current cost
matters: current cost reflects current strength; high-water investment matches
existing population accounting and can remain higher after cost reduction. Do
not call it decided. A Prince can have no rank ceiling yet still run out of budget,
coins or global capacity. Temporarily delegated command must not make an army
count under two owners or allow ownership splitting to bypass the Lord ceiling.

Enforce chosen basis at recruitment, troop ownership transfer/acceptance, level-up
cost reconciliation and admin overrides; handle rank demotion and existing over-
ceiling records without deleting troops. Retain safe control/recovery/return for
existing soldiers while disallowing further growth if that policy is chosen.
Level-up denial currently has population consequences in KOMEEvents; don't forget
native hired NPC health and packet sync when introducing a personal cap.

Population delegation is not current company delegation. A company grant permits
temporary movement/command; it does not allocate unspent population to recruit.
Old record allocationTileId/allocationFaction/allocationPlayer are explicitly
cleared for canonical faction-bank hires. Reusing those fields as though they
already store Crown budgets would corrupt provenance. Reuse bank transaction and
audit service, but add only minimal owner-chosen allocation records in a later
implementation. Approval/consumption/revocation and repeat receipts need tests.
Do not duplicate faction population by crediting both the bank and player wallet.

Global/performance caps: current populationCapValue clamps AVAILABLE BANK payouts.
It can reduce future available credits, but active troops remain and spending
creates room for future credits; it is not a living entity cap. Bounded marker/
actor lists and network/task queues are local safety guards, not army limits.

active server/faction/company limit after load testing. No unlimited Prince army.

R. OVERALL ASSESSMENT AND STRONGEST RECOMMENDATIONS
==================================================

Keep the permanent ladder, political office separation, native pledge/alignment,
NPC superior identity, current direct report paths, distinct faction-provenance
Knight service, bound native property, technical activation recovery and transactional
strategic recruitment. They are useful foundations, not obsolete RPG machinery.

Remove the ordinary survival restrictions and old baseline permission presentation.
Replace rank privilege reads with derived current standing, while preserving legacy
completion history. Separate normal equipment use from specialist manufacture and
exceptional artifacts. Fix client projection parity before trusting a cleaner UI.

Treat population authorization and sovereignty as the main multiplayer boundaries.
Knight participation is not personal strategic hiring; Lord permission is not free
population; Prince rank is not a fief; King office is not permanent promotion;
capture participation is not unilateral war declaration. The present bank cap,
company delegation and NPC King do not secretly implement the missing mechanics.

Integrate a compact privileges strip into the existing service/rank book. Show
unlocked standing separately from temporarily unavailable actions, expose actual
blockers on hover/denial and keep current world service prominent. Preserve native
items, cultural titles, map focus and concise tracker behavior.

Polish the real Serf/Knight/Command journey before adding Hunt or higher offices.
Most remaining coherence work can reuse current classes. The cleanest evolution
is fewer contradictory permission authorities, clearer genuine responsibilities
and more reliable native-world interactions, not more framework, screens or flags.
