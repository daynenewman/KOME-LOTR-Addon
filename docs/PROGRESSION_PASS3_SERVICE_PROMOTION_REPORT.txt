E. MASTER SERVICE AND SERF -> KNIGHT
===================================
IMPLEMENTED AND TESTED; LIVE ACCEPTANCE PENDING.
A Serf can serve an eligible adult native ordinary civilian of their current
faction, including farmer-only hire traders. Selection checks identity, native
faction, civilian/occupation role, recruitment/hired state, encounter exclusion,
friendliness/native interaction eligibility, shared server world and range.
New service requires native faction territory, not a fort, capital or rare noble.
Returning to an already accepted Master is not treated as new location admission.
The Master remains personally UNRANKED and does not become a noble by conferring
the player's standing on the faction's behalf.

One active duty is saved. Completed types are not repeated. Live random selection
filters unavailable local escort/defense work and persists the accepted objective,
items, target/token and destination. Reopening cannot reroll the accepted duty.
Provisioning and occupational materials are alternative delivery experiences:
the live selector excludes the second when the first has been completed.
The normal three-duty journey therefore uses other world interactions too.

No one-duty-per-calendar-day limit remains. Assignment/replay checks and short
technical recovery throttles remain. The separately established betrayal lockout
and strategic daily population/movement cadence were not redesigned.

Final completion/report invokes KOMEServicePromotionService.report at the current
Master. Three non-forced duty credits plus 150 current alignment confer Knight,
end active Master service, retain history and issue the regional recommendation.
If alignment is short, all three credits remain and recognition is available here
later without a fourth duty. No final-assignment alignment issuance lock remains.
The existing optional parting gift is once-only and is not an admission/rank gate.

G. DUTY/SERVICE INVENTORY AND REAL VARIETY
========================================
Master pool: provisioning; occupation-specific material assistance; courier;
escort; property recovery; local defense. Provisioning uses existing culturally
appropriate, attainable food/drink catalogs. Profession reuses genuine occupation
and material catalogs but remains a delivery activity; it is not advertised as a
new crafting simulation. At most one of those delivery categories is selected in
the normal live journey. Courier requires a bound message, actual recipient and
return report. Escort uses a real charge and verified destination. Recovery uses
bound property. Defense requires real protected-NPC survival and participation.

Knight pool: settlement defense; border incursion; dangerous escort; relief;
stolen property; native invasion. Field objectives, travel, recipient aid, inventory
handover, participation and loss conditions differ. Native enemy/faction/territory
tables and feasible destination checks are reused. Three different types qualify;
players are not assigned an identical exact sequence. Saved offers and objectives
prevent reopening rerolls. Infeasible types fall back to other eligible types.
If genuine local evidence is insufficient, issuance explains that service cannot
be arranged and preserves credit rather than inventing a settlement or target.

Genuine gameplay failures retain their existing objective consequences. One failure
does not erase unrelated reported service. A dead courier recipient cancels the
undeliverable duty after bound-message recovery; it does not earn delivery credit.
Technical activation/spawn failures preserve credit and offer retry/reassignment.
