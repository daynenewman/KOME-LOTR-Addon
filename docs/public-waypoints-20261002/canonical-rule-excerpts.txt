KINGS OF MIDDLE-EARTH | RULES SIMPLIFICATION & MOD AUTOMATION PLAN 2.1 Concepts that should not be reintroduced without a new decision • No population cap or active-population cap for now. • No defensive population. • No population from roads, farms, or waypoint captures unless a future rule explicitly adds it. • No Encirclement population shutdown. • No faction-destruction objective snapshot. • No surrender system. • No automatic server-triggered finale. • No old Civic/Trade/Military tier tables once Section 4.1 is implemented. 3. Progression and Kingship 3.1 Separate progression tier from political office Keep the five-stage progression concept, quotas, class quests, and optional advancements. The final progression stage should not be the exclusive political office of King. Recommended internal model Use a stable internal progression tier such as Wanderer -> Serf -> Knight -> Lord -> Endgame. Each faction may display its own lore-appropriate title for those tiers. King is a separate political office held by one eligible player. • Finishing progression is never blocked because another player already holds the throne. • Endgame players keep earned progression permissions even if they are not King. • The King receives ruler permissions such as diplomacy, capital management, alliance invitations, unit delegation, and other high-level faction actions. • Use an admin command for kingship transfer in V1. More elaborate succession can be added later if needed. • Faction changes should preserve historical achievements while resetting only the progression pieces the server deliberately chooses to reset. 3.2 Mod requirements • Server-side progression record with completed quotas, quests, advancements, internal tier, display title, and permissions. • One recognized ruler UUID per faction. • Admin commands to assign/remove the ruler and repair progression state. • Central permission API so other KOME systems ask one service whether a player may perform a progression-gated action. 4. Alliances, Diplomacy, and Delegation 4.1 Canonical alliance format An alliance or relation change is formed through a King-to-King invitation. One faction King chooses the requested LOTR-style relation level - Neutral, Friends, or Allies - and sends the request to the other faction King. If the recipient accepts, the addon updates the relationship for both factions. • Accepted relations make the factions' waypoint access follow the agreed relationship. • Armies may pass through each other's territory when the accepted relationship permits military passage. Population, Normal Builds, and Defensive Builds 5.1 Population vocabulary Term Meaning Daily Population Rate How much population a faction gains at each daily population tick. It is generated only by approved normal builds currently controlled by that faction. Available Population Banked, unspent population. Hiring a unit spends from this pool. There is no cap for now. Active Population For display/auditing only: the total population cost represented by currently living hired units. There is no active-population cap for now. Normal Build A player-built structure approved to generate population rate. Defensive Build A registered fortress/defensive structure used by the siege system. It does not generate population rate. Its approved build time instead strengthens the gate guarding its defensive segment. 5.2 Normal-build population rate Population comes from building in the game. For every 10 approved hours of eligible design/build work on a normal build, that build contributes +1 population per day to the controlling faction. • Example: 105 approved build-hours contributes 10.5 population/day. These are campaign/governance states, not battlefield surrender. Outside war, existing capital-location requirements may apply. 10. Uneven Player Activity and Kingless Factions Faction viability should not depend on every faction having the same real-world player count. The goal is not to give inactive factions artificial player-level power; it is to prevent them from becoming empty map colors. 10.1 Design principles • Activity should increase how many things a faction can do, not guarantee that every individual battle is unwinnable for a smaller faction. • Physical logistics are the main natural balancing tool: an army sent across Middle-earth is not simultaneously defending home territory. • Native/institutional defense and kingless stewardship may preserve a faction when its real player is absent, but should not permanently transfer ownership to an active ally. • Any kingless/inactivity system must respect the same population, company, diplomacy, and siege rules as normal factions where practical. 10.2 Kingless defense - V1 direction • Provide admin/game tools to ensure a kingless faction's legitimate hired-unit companies stationed for defense are equipped and registered correctly before an impending siege. • If stewardship is used, grant temporary controller authority without changing native faction identity or population ownership. • End stewardship automatically when a valid ruler returns or diplomatic/war conditions no longer permit it. • Keep all stewardship actions logged. 11.
