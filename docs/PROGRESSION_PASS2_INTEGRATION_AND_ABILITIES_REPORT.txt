A. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
====================
The original progression feature branch now includes origin/dev's KOM-18 history
through 6d1669a29c572161fa8b14b0779734eb63323af9. Pass 1 and the older unpublished
Knight/Lordship work were backed up and checkpointed first. No work was discarded,
no existing stash was applied or changed, and nothing was pushed.

A modest extension of the existing permission registry derives standing eligibility
from KOMEPlayerProgression.getCanonicalRank(). There is no second rank, unlock save
ledger, political authority service, recruitment service or battle resolver.

New faction Build intents require Serf, new formal battle entry requires Knight,
new company ownership/delegation requires Lord, and new Campaign hiring retains
Lord plus actual Crown authority. Existing legitimate command, battle maintenance,
retreat and recovery are preserved. A new hostile player order outside a declared
war requires recognized ruler/staff authority; ordinary commanders cannot use that
path to originate a capture-created war. KOM-18's timer/victory/publication is intact.

The Rank page keeps current service first and adds a compact current/next privilege
list with item icons and concise hover reasons. Rank unlock and current availability
are separate. Exact original-start return is implemented where character creation
saved and applied a valid built-in waypoint; native travel rules remain mandatory.

Standard equipment remains native LOTR gameplay. Exceptional equipment, advanced
manufacture, Warfare rewards, royal warrants, troop ceilings, fiefs and capstones
were not invented. King remains a political office, independent of Prince standing.

C. CONQUEST INTEGRATION
=======================
KOMEConflictMovementService validates legal hostile terminal arrival and Campaign
commitment. KOMEConflictService owns the canonical per-tile ConflictRecord.
KOMEOrdinaryBattleLifecycleService owns episode continuity, server-active response
time, reinforcement non-reset, abandonment and genuine hostile recovery evidence.
KOMEOrdinaryBattleResolutionService preflights current military, timer, tile,
fortification, survivor and movement-hold authority before publication.
KOMEOrdinaryBattleOwnershipResolver selects the eligible surviving faction using
default-owner/allied coalition/continuous-participation rules and deterministic
tie-breaking. KOMEWorldData.claimTile remains the canonical ownership mutation seam.
KOMEWarService owns capture history/war state; KOMEDiplomacyService owns diplomacy.
KOMEEvents.processCampaignTick invokes the merged lifecycle/resolution/recovery.

Timer expiry alone is not victory while hostile committed factions remain. Retreat
and abandonment do not manufacture a capture. Fortified/Encirclement contexts defer
to siege authority; ordinary resolution cannot complete uncaptured Siege Complexes.
Recovery uses versioned health, durable intents and WAL acknowledgment/replay,
not progression flags or client victory requests. +5 current-HP recovery is the
configured contributor policy, not a new reward or population adjustment here.

Manual claim service/packet remains a read-only denial even for high rank, legacy
flags, disabled progression and replay. Tile Command's Conquest action remains
disabled with the canonical resolution/peaceful-transfer explanation. No duplicate
victory button or progression-owned battle referee was added.

Significant authority interaction found after integration:
KOM-18's legitimate hostile capture records history through recordHostileCapture,
which can create a capture-origin war when one does not yet exist. Changing the
resolver would risk its durable publication/recovery semantics. Instead,
KOMECompanyCommandAuthority.newMilitaryOperationDenial is called by the actual
new-order/preview validation in KOMECommandTroops.validateCompanyMove. A native-
hostile target outside declared war requires recognized ruler or server staff.
An ordinary Lord/Prince commander can issue otherwise legal wartime orders, but
rank/ownership alone cannot initiate the operation that originates a new war.
Already queued orders and already committed conflicts keep their accepted authority;
there is no retroactive cancellation that strands existing troops.

Ruler peaceful offers/acceptance and staff ownership corrections remain separate
legitimate operations. Recognized office is read from KOMERulerService, not the
displayed King title or Prince rank. Native relations, passage, unit classification,
movement geometry and battlefield participation are still domain decisions.

Regression coverage retained/run:
- Legal hostile capture, unchanged-owner defender outcome and idempotent history.
- Timer expiry with hostile forces still present; reinforcement and continuity.
- Formal retreat, abandonment, recovery and later new episodes.
- Multiple allied attackers/default-owner selection and continuity tie-breaks.
- Fortified deferral, legal siege timer/state contracts and no ordinary bypass.
- Emergency Defense native ownership, pending/active defender contributions.
- Peaceful ruler transfer, staff correction and forged/replayed manual claims.
- ConflictData v3 save/reload, Join Battle receipts, health/WAL startup/replay.
- New Lord pre-war order denial, ruler/staff exception and declared-war acceptance.

Unclaimed land does not acquire a new player claim route. The hostile-arrival path
requires an authoritative defender; ambiguous/unclaimed targets fail safely under
the existing domain rules. Staff/political ownership operations remain separate.
This is not a new designed civilian occupation mechanic.

Outstanding contributor follow-ups remain: KOM-91 stale egress between battles,
KOM-92 Emergency Defense protection and KOM-20 battlefield interference/referee
acceptance. Full multi-complex siege completion is not implemented by this pass.
Existing contributor live observations were read; they are not this pass's tests.

D. IMPLEMENTED RANK-ABILITY ARCHITECTURE
=======================================
Reused: KOMECanonicalRankService, KOMEPlayerProgression, progression rank enum,
KOMEProgressionPermissions, KOMEProgressionPermissionRegistry, existing rank/service
summaries, KOMEProgressionAutoCompleter, packet 9 and Pass 1 client lifecycle fences.

Added one production class: KOMEProgressionAbilityPolicy.
Added a small Ability enum inside the existing permission registry. Each ability
has one stable ID, minimum canonical rank, title, description, native item icon
key, appointment/context dependency metadata and enforcement-site inventory.
The old advancement Gate inventory remains intact.

Ability IDs and minimum standing:
build.submit - Serf
travel.start - Serf
equipment.standard - Knight
battle.join - Knight
company.command - Lord
campaign.recruit - Lord
company.orders - Lord

Rank inheritance uses the canonical enum order; Prince inherits all these floors.
King is absent from that enum. Reads do not create a missing progression record.
Historical completion markers and disabled legacy progression do not satisfy a
new ability rank floor. Explicit existing server administrative hiring authority
is still supported, but is never read from client requests or presented as earned
standing. No persistent unlock flags or rank migration were added.

Outcome structure: stable ID, rankUnlocked, Availability, reason, optional target.
LOCKED means the rank floor is missing. UNAVAILABLE means standing is earned but
an identified live requirement is missing. AVAILABLE is used for an exact legal
starting return. CONTEXT_REQUIRED truthfully requires a selected unit, company,
destination, item, Build or deployment before final authorization can be decided.
Unknown action context is never presented as a definite grant.

Campaign rank/pledge/Crown checks are shared with actual recruitment through
KOMEProgressionPermissions.campaignRecruitmentDenial. The domain recruitment
transaction still checks governance, native hiring, actual costs, legal location,
deployment, bank debit, company admission and rollback. A positive bank balance
does not promise enough population for every unit. A zero balance has the real
resource denial; no arbitrary troop price or budget is guessed for the book.

Battle availability queries the real KOMEJoinBattleService over active conflicts.
Company availability reads KOMECompanyCommandAuthority. Destination-specific route
checks remain in CommandTroops. Existing unpledged/foreign command delegation
contracts are preserved: the generic pledge warning does not override an already
legal company command appointment. Equipment is delegated to native/item rules.

Synchronization: packet 9 appends at most seven outcomes with bounded IDs/counts,
duplicate-ID and contradictory-state rejection; collections are detached/immutable.
Packet 5 carries Build submission eligibility and reason. Existing packet IDs are
unchanged. Both build and wire protocol are 1.0.9-integration-g9; g8 is rejected.
Client abilities publish only after accepted viewer, connection, revision and
dimension checks, together with existing book data. Disconnect/world transitions
clear them. No duplicate client rank was introduced.

The existing 100-tick progression check compares outcome explanations alongside
rank, pledge, alignment, mode, assignments and history. Changed appointment,
population/company/conflict context yields a refreshed snapshot without publishing
every unrelated tick. Login, promotion/admin, faction and dimension lifecycles
remain. Final actions always revalidate the server state. Availability can be a
few seconds old between snapshots; it is never an authority token.

F. RECRUITMENT COMPATIBILITY
============================
Ordinary LOTR followers retain native follower/travel/ambient behavior and remain
ORDINARY. Native combat hiring still charges canonical faction population exactly
once through ordinary registration; farmhands retain native price and zero population.
It does not enroll proximity-observed followers into companies or formal conflict.
Foreign ordinary bodyguards retain Pass 1's native-eligibility distinction.

CAMPAIGN identity remains explicit, persisted and exclusive to legal strategic
admission. No ordinary mobilization/retirement operation is invented here. Both
combat classes use the same faction bank/cost model. Mounted costs, overrides,
fixed-point fractions, permanent investment, rollback and native coin charging
remain. New rank checks precede economic/gameplay effects.

Knight cannot recruit a new Campaign army through a historical achievement. Lord
eligibility is insufficient to spend shared population. Non-ruler authority remains
unavailable until real royal warrants/budgets exist. Ruler and trusted server admin
exceptions remain separate from rank inheritance. Existing companies, legitimate
transfers/control maintenance, Emergency Defense, retreat, health reconstruction
and WAL recovery remain safe; no retroactive debit, refund, reclassification or
disbanding occurs. New recipient appointments now require Lord, but already
established lower-rank ownership/delegation remains maintainable.

G. GUI CHANGES
===============
The existing native LOTR book panel and Rank/Service & Rank tab remain.
Layout: standing line; current service/activity; promotion requirements and
existing relationship/history context; current privileges; next-standing privileges.
The current service remains above the privilege inventory. Existing Show Me,
relationship footer, advancement browsing and rank scrolling remain.

Seven supported rows maximum, with only the immediate next rank previewed.
Wanderer sees two Serf future rows; Serf sees two current/two Knight future rows;
Knight sees four current/three Lord future rows; Lord/Prince see seven inherited
rows without fictional Prince/Fief/capstone content. The legacy baseline checklist
remains hidden. Warfare scaffolding is not another advancement tree in this tab.

Rows use 20-pixel height, vanilla/native item icons (paper, compass, iron armor,
iron sword, map, gold nugget), existing parchment/red/brown visual formatting,
earned checkmarks, locked boxes and an amber unavailable mark. Titles trim to the
available width; hover text contains the full title, standing/state reason and
short scope. Tooltip hit testing respects the clipped viewport. Content-height
calculations include both headings, rows and the existing relationship footer.

Unlocked-but-unavailable remains an earned privilege with a specific denial,
rather than a rank lock. Context-required rows avoid claiming complete access
before selection. Build controls use server-authored rank eligibility and the
same concise rank denial; the disabled Conquest button remains canonical.

Only the available exact starting-return row has a new direct click action, using
the existing request packet and complete server revalidation. Native map behavior
and other travel permissions are not broadened into a universal ladder.

Limits: pure layout calculations, packet/lifecycle execution and existing real
GuiScreen Build mouse tests ran. The Rank page itself was not drawn in Minecraft;
actual icon rendering, long cultural titles, hover position, mouse scrolling and
GUI scale/clipping need connected-client inspection. No screenshot/live visual
acceptance or performance claim is made.
